# AyudaChain
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Beneficiaries, Barangay Officers, DSWD Officers, Disbursing Officers and independent read-only Auditors in one DSWD Field Office.
## Product Purpose
Trace disaster cash assistance from funding through household claims, human review, computed entitlements, published lists, scheduled payout, recipient authorization, once-only recorded handover, private recipient confirmation and independent audit.
## Operating Context
Mobile household use and task-oriented staff work. Philippine peso amounts; Asia/Manila dates. Existing Next.js frontend and authoritative FastAPI/PostgreSQL MVP in this checkout.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Use existing persisted workflows and server projections. Dedicated role URLs organize screens, not authentication. Selected accounts are claimed and unverified; invented records only. National ID checks and SMS notices are explicitly simulated. Chain verification is actual and fail-closed. All personal evidence stays off-chain. Decisions and histories are append-only. No offline payout. No schema, contract or hashing changes in the redesign.
## Brand Commitments
AyudaChain; trustworthy public-service language, clear typography, restrained color, understandable state and prominent next action. Focus exclusively on /mvp.
## Evidence on Hand
Existing originals, records and local-chain commitments; docs/product/prd.md and settled process/role documents. Never manufacture records or chain evidence to fill a screen.
## Product Principles
Human decisions; truthful evidence; privacy by role; one recorded handover per entitlement; next actions grounded in current records.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard access, labelled inputs, visible focus, responsive desktop/mobile screens and reassuring household guidance.
