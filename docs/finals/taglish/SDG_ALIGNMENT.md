# Aling SDGs ang applicable?

Basahin ang [complete bilingual SDG guide](../SDG_ALIGNMENT.md): ready-to-paste slide copy, code evidence, limits at answers sa judges.

**Main: SDG16 — Strong institutions, target16.6.** May connected evidence, retained decisions, recipient report at actual integrity check para mas madaling i-review ang disaster-assistance records.

**Supporting: SDG1 — No poverty, target1.5.** Disaster relief ang context. Hindi claim na napatunayan na nating bumaba ang poverty.

**Supporting: SDG10 — Reduced inequalities, target10.2.** Assisted registration, optional phone/QR at English/Taglish help reduce some barriers. Hindi universal access: payment ID/PIN/checker and connectivity limits remain.

**Optional context: SDG13, target13.1.** Disaster-response setting lang; walang climate prediction/prevention o measured resilience impact.

Spoken answer: “SDG16 ang pinaka-direct dahil accountability ang core. Related sa SDG1 ang disaster assistance at sa SDG10 ang assisted access. Proposed contribution ito, hindi measured impact o UN endorsement.”
