## Five-minute emergency study sheet

**0:00–0:40 — What it is.** “AyudaChain connects funding evidence, household decisions, documented amounts, recorded cash handover and recipient follow-up. It lets an independent reviewer check whether committed evidence still matches its chain fingerprint.” It records cash; it does not move money. For the synthetic wage example: 75% of ₱435 is ₱326.25, rounded up to ₱330; 15 documented days produce ₱4,950. This is not an official current wage-rate claim.

**0:40–1:20 — The three separate facts.** A saved handover says an officer recorded the action. A recipient answer says what that recipient reported. A current matching hash says the committed evidence agrees with a fresh chain read. None substitutes for the other two. A false original or collusion can still pass a hash check.

**1:20–2:00 — Built versus pending.** Actual: document uploads/extraction, PostgreSQL versions/audits, human review, calculations, sessions, code/PIN workflow, once-only handover, private follow-up, local EVM commitments/reads and durable recovery. Simulated: National ID, notice SMS and any labelled accelerated business clock. Pending: sign-in, operational privacy, live integrations, official procedure/signature alignment, production hosting/key/network governance, offline payout and full liquidation.

**2:00–2:40 — The hardest security answer.** “There is no authentication on this MVP. Active actors can be impersonated. We use invented records only. Stored workflow scopes are useful engineering boundaries, but real data requires authenticated principals, secure sessions/recovery and privacy/security approval.” Never claim the other branch’s authentication is on this branch.

**2:40–3:20 — Why blockchain.** “A separately retained commitment can expose changes to already committed content outside the database administrator’s own log. Our local chain demonstrates the protocol; independent governance is still required. We will compare signed logs/timestamping before selecting production infrastructure.” Local EVM, chain ID 31337; no public Polygon deployment or purchased gas.

**3:20–4:00 — Inclusion/outages.** QR is optional; manual reference entry works. Phone optional at registration; assisted no-phone payout uses a connected staff device, private PIN and distinct checker. Accepted photo ID is still needed before payment under this prototype. Offline payout is unavailable. On a lost handover reply, inspect the same receipt before safe replay; never repeat physical cash.

**4:00–4:40 — Business.** Proposed institutional implementation/training/support, no beneficiary fee or percentage of relief funds. No validated selling price, customer, ROI or savings study. The hypothetical model has invented assumptions explicitly labelled; quote actual supplier/workload evidence before making an offer. Government adoption needs authorized procedure, privacy, IT, audit and procurement review.

**4:40–5:00 — Close under pressure.** “Here is what our code and current evidence show; here is what remains pending.” Ask for a supervised synthetic validation partner and agreed readiness metrics. If a live check fails, read the failure aloud and use the labelled recorded fallback. Do not apologize for refusing to invent evidence.

## Business model and adoption


### Q001. Who is the customer?

**Spoken:** The proposed buyer is an institution running a defined relief workflow; the household is the service user.

**Explain:** Begin with one authorized Field Office validation partner. Program owners, cash staff, auditors, IT and privacy officers have different acceptance needs, so an enthusiastic end user alone cannot approve adoption.

**Evidence:** [Approved MVP scope and assurance](../product/prd.md); [Adoption/readiness boundaries](../guides/local-mvp.md).

**Limit:** No signed customer, procurement award or willingness-to-pay study is claimed.

**Likely follow-up:** Who controls the budget and operational approval?


### Q002. What exactly would you sell?

**Spoken:** A possible service is implementation, training, support and accountable operations around the open-source software.

**Explain:** Scope deliverables separately: procedure mapping, secure configuration, evidence migration, training, incident response and maintenance. The software license does not make hosting, security work or support free.

**Evidence:** [MIT license](../../LICENSE); [Local operations/recovery](../guides/local-mvp.md).

**Limit:** This is a business proposal, not a launched service or binding offer.

**Likely follow-up:** Which responsibilities stay with the agency?


### Q003. Why would an agency change its current process?

**Spoken:** Only if a measured pilot shows easier evidence retrieval and defensible review without disrupting assistance.

**Explain:** Start from existing forms and pain points. Observe how staff reconcile a funding document, decision and receipt; measure the effort before proposing replacement. Keep institutional authority with the agency.

**Evidence:** [Approved MVP scope and assurance](../product/prd.md); [Adoption/readiness boundaries](../guides/local-mvp.md).

**Limit:** No measured time saving or government demand is established.

**Likely follow-up:** What baseline would the pilot collect?


### Q004. Do citizens pay a fee?

**Spoken:** Our proposed model charges the institution, with no beneficiary fee or percentage of assistance.

**Explain:** Avoid incentives tied to reducing approvals, classifying more fraud or charging desperate recipients. A service agreement should state ownership, access, exit and continuity obligations.

**Evidence:** [MIT license](../../LICENSE); [Approved MVP scope and assurance](../product/prd.md).

**Limit:** This is a proposed commercial principle, not an existing contract.

**Likely follow-up:** How will you prevent hidden service deductions?


### Q005. What is the first adoption step?

**Spoken:** A supervised synthetic workflow review with authorized program, audit, IT and privacy staff.

**Explain:** Bring realistic invented cases, then compare decisions, documents and exceptions with current procedures. Agree readiness gates before collecting private records or running a live payout.

**Evidence:** [Approved MVP scope and assurance](../product/prd.md); [DSWD official issuance register](https://www.dswd.gov.ph/issuance/).

**Limit:** No agency partner or lawful processing arrangement is assumed.

**Likely follow-up:** What would make you stop the pilot?


### Q006. Who are your adoption champions and blockers?

**Spoken:** Program staff may champion simpler records; cash, audit, privacy, IT and procurement teams must also agree.

**Explain:** A role interview should expose duplicate entry, approval authority, staffing gaps, retention, incident ownership and what evidence is acceptable. These constraints shape a pilot more than a polished dashboard.

**Evidence:** [Approved MVP scope and assurance](../product/prd.md); [Adoption/readiness boundaries](../guides/local-mvp.md).

**Limit:** These are stakeholder hypotheses, not completed interview findings.

**Likely follow-up:** Have those people validated the workflow?


### Q007. How do you avoid vendor lock-in?

**Spoken:** Retain inspectable data, originals, protocol documentation and an independent verifier; define an exit plan.

**Explain:** The current CLI can recompute authorized evidence without the database or signer. A production service still needs usable exports, data dictionaries, secure handover and a tested transition process.

**Evidence:** [Signer-free independent verifier](../../contracts/scripts/verify_evidence.js); [Accepted evidence compatibility ADR](../adr/2026-10-10-mvp-evidence-protocol-and-registry.md); [MIT license](../../LICENSE).

**Limit:** Formal institutional export/report workflows are deferred.

**Likely follow-up:** Can another provider operate it after you leave?


### Q008. How do you support an agency with limited staff?

**Spoken:** Reduce unclear steps and explain the permitted next action, but never bypass an independent human check.

**Explain:** Current task queues, operations counts and role-specific error recovery help staff find pending work. Assisted payout still requires eligible separate staff; missing independence means hold and escalate.

## Blockchain implementation and gas


### Q041. Why not use only a database?

**Spoken:** A database is essential; an independently retained commitment can add an outside comparison point.

**Explain:** Privileged operators can potentially rewrite local records/history. A reviewer who independently retains the chain reference can detect changed committed content. If the operator also controls and can discard the whole local chain, the trust benefit is limited.

**Evidence:** [Actual evidence registry](../../contracts/contracts/AyudaChainEvidenceRegistry.sol); [Accepted evidence compatibility ADR](../adr/2026-10-10-mvp-evidence-protocol-and-registry.md).

**Limit:** The current local Hardhat node is not independently governed infrastructure.

**Likely follow-up:** Would signed logs or timestamping meet the same need?

### Journey 1 — A household uses its own device

**Starting situation:** Invented DFC-2026-9103 submits during open covered intake. A Barangay Officer disputes affectedness/category or the sender misses the deadline.

6. If approved after legitimate review, amount inputs/publication/session/recipient authorization/identity checks lead to the same once-only handover and private outcome as Journey 1.

### Journey 4 — Failed comparison, later clearance and uncertain response

**Starting situation:** Invented DFC-2026-9104 has a published entitlement but the payer cannot pass the photo-ID comparison.

2. DSWD reviews the refusal and records permitted clearance with evidence. A distinct session created after clearance is required; it can be later the same day under current rules.
3. Current list/input/session/linkage authority is rechecked. An eligible recipient opens a fresh attempt under the same stable transaction ID, not a second entitlement.
4. The later payer passes the actual comparison, presents fixed cash and obtains the current recipient code. The final review dialog repeats household/amount.
5. Suppose the response is lost after saving. Staff reconnect and read that exact transaction. If the receipt is present, cash is already recorded. Replaying the same request recovers acknowledgment without another physical payment.
6. The worker reconciles the original signed evidence candidate after its own interruption; proof recovery never repeats the business handover.
7. Recipient follow-up and independent current verification continue with the retained failed/cleared/paid history.

### Journey 5 — Funding correction, reduced cash report and independent audit

**Starting situation:** Invented DFC-2026-9105 receives a recorded handover; a funding transcription error and an adverse recipient report require review.

1. DSWD detects a mis-transcribed release amount against the retained original. A linked reasoned reversal/replacement corrects the recorded ledger; the earlier entry remains.
2. Actual chain correction commits the signed pair atomically. A failure remains pending/failed and does not delete the database correction or create a second independent release.
3. The recipient privately reports receiving less, with the actual reported amount. The system opens/reopens one linked grievance. This is a statement to investigate, not an automatic finding of theft.
4. DSWD records human investigation/resolution. Later revised answers and reopening preserve prior answers/resolutions. A later full answer does not silently close the grievance.
5. If calculation inputs later change, the paid receipt stays fixed; any difference is informational. No second payout or reversal follows automatically.
6. Auditor opens permitted originals, ledger lineage, claim/basis/list/handovers and private case history. Access is logged separately before disclosure; the Auditor cannot edit or mark the business records.
7. Independent recomputation uses exact authorized envelope/original bytes and the pinned registry. Match proves content continuity only. Mismatch, absence, unreachable chain and missing material remain distinct.

**Completion:** Retained correction, locked handover, private complaint/resolution history and independently inspectable integrity evidence. **Limit:** The software cannot establish physical truth, compel repayment or conduct an official audit by itself. **Evidence:** [funds](../../backend/app/services/funds.py), [outcomes](../../backend/app/services/outcomes.py), [independent verifier](../../contracts/scripts/verify_evidence.js).

### ROI sensitivity that does not invent savings

Assume, solely for illustration, 1,000 records/month, baseline work 8 minutes/record, future work 5 minutes/record and staff-time value ₱250/hour. The assumed time difference is `1,000 × (8−5) ÷ 60 = 50 hours`; its scenario value is `50 × ₱250 = ₱12,500`. Against the hypothetical ₱18,000 recurring fee alone, the scenario net is **−₱5,500/month**, before implementation and other costs. There is no positive ROI claim.

At that assumed labor value, the fee alone would require 72 saved hours/month to break even, or 4.32 minutes per 1,000 records. With implementation and other costs, more benefit is necessary.
