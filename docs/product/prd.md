## Problem, users and value

Disaster cash assistance needs a traceable basis for funding, list inclusion, computed amounts, recorded handovers and recipients' subsequent reports. Mutable records and disconnected evidence make later review difficult. AyudaChain gives households and staff a shared workflow and gives an independent Auditor retrievable evidence whose committed versions can be checked against the chain.

The intended users are Beneficiaries, Barangay Officers, DSWD Officers, Disbursing Officers and read-only Auditors. Scope is one DSWD Field Office and direct DSWD cash payout. One household is the assistance unit: one entitlement and at most one recorded handover per disaster. This deliberately simplifies FACED's separately living families; it is not a statement of statutory eligibility.

The value is an inspectable record of **what was recorded, by which claimed actor, on which evidence and amount/list versions**, plus private human follow-up. Hashes cannot establish affectedness, genuine original documents, authentic officers, physical cash receipt, complete recording of real-world events, absence of collusion or cuts collected outside the site.

## Journeys and recovery

1. **Funding and preparation.** DSWD uploads the actual release order and available NTA/supporting files; reviews document type/reference/date, amount, purpose, area and printed signatories against real extraction or manual fields and explicitly confirms. Mark machine-read fields; retain extracted and confirmed amounts plus override indicator. Words/figures disagreement warns and remains evidence, rather than blocking human confirmation; unparseable or absent wording stays unknown. Save/count funding authority and required anchor intents; show independent failures. Correct errors through an atomic linked reversal/replacement with reason. DSWD separately records operation authority/basis, coverage and response duration, and explicitly opens intake; a release does not open claims or prove available cash.
2. **Registry and claim.** Register household identity/members/address, distinct account/contact linkage and evidence at any time. Residence starts unconfirmed. A no-ID household can register/claim but needs documented DSWD field checking and an accepted government photo ID before payment. Submission freezes registry/claim versions and a response deadline. Closure stops new claims and resubmissions, not existing review/referrals.
3. **Barangay to DSWD.** Eligible officers append attestation/category opinions and required negative/dispute/change reasons. Positive endorsement attests residence and affectedness, not identity. Two active officers are needed for endorsement; own-household/assister recusals apply. Punong Barangay submits fixed responded versions with an uploaded resolution. Fewer than two officers/all barred routes immediately with `no_eligible_endorser`; deadline routes `no_barangay_response` or `no_barangay_submission`, retaining actual negative/withdrawn responses. Fallback never manufactures sender, endorsement or resolution. Anchor failure never withholds review.
4. **Human decision and entitlement.** Local automatic checks flag person in two households, reused ID, phone across more than three households, already approved/handed-over household/disaster, and aid in another barangay for that disaster, including name/address near-matches. Missing fields do not match each other. DSWD reviews risk order, approves/final category, declines/reason or refers/question/fresh deadline. No independent reviewer means hold/escalate. Bulk approval requires every claim endorsed, unflagged and category undisputed. Decline permits one new-information resubmission while intake open. Approval creates a stable entitlement even without inputs: amount remains absent/awaiting, never zero.
5. **Amount and publication.** DSWD records evidence for every wage/category-day or fixed-category input. One disaster uses one documented mode per version. Wage formula: `ceil((0.75 × daily wage) / 10 pesos) × 10 pesos × category days`. No automatic wage feed, invented day mapping, fixed default grant or legacy ceiling. Publish a cumulative immutable barangay list only with computed amounts and its own confirmed matching anchor. Earlier lists remain history. Household sees own amount/basis; confirmed residents and barangay see restricted columns. Publication sends no payout notice.
6. **Session and payout.** DSWD schedules date/site, published list/payable members and named officers, triggering linkless notices; opens on the session's Manila date. Recipient opens a fresh attempt under one stable transaction ID and receives a short-lived code only then. Officer compares accepted photo ID/person, presents computed amount/cash; recipient counts before revealing code. Handover rechecks current eligibility, entitlement/input/list/session versions, active assignment/date/recusal and unused code; records/consumes once atomically. No code, no handover. A failed ID comparison stops/holds with reason; DSWD clearance allows a fresh attempt in a distinct later session, retaining stop/history/transaction ID. “Later” is after clearance, not necessarily a different calendar date. Ordinary unused expiry can retry in a still-valid session.
7. **Assisted recipient.** No-phone recipient supplies private PIN after ID checking and authorizes opening/code on the officer's device. PIN is separate from payout code, stored hashed, never revealed/logged/texted. Two eligible assigned officers are required before handover; missing independent checker means hold/escalate. Checker is neither payer nor recipient-household member and records the recipient's exit answer, including later after closure. Unexpected missing exit check after handover leads to follow-up/unconfirmed, never reversal.
8. **Private outcome and audit.** After recorded handover recipient answers full/less/not received. No answer at handover + 72 hours creates neutral unconfirmed, without penalty/grievance. Late/revised answers remain possible after closure. Adverse answer/report creates or reopens one transaction-linked private grievance; keep earlier answers, reviews, resolutions and reopenings. Later full answer does not close a grievance automatically. DSWD records human resolution; Auditor inspects evidence/current chain matches privately. None of these events awards another payment or undoes cash.

The Auditor's funds exceptions are not anchored, document mismatch, corrected, amount overridden, words/figures disagree and duplicate document offered. Full-scope exceptions also include officer endorsement/category/assistance/relative/deadline figures, bulk approvals, changed categories/inputs, approved amounts above recorded balance, stopped/assisted/adverse/unconfirmed payouts, post-handover challenges, grievances, fallback snapshots, stale authorizations, pending publication and paid differences. Show denominators and evidence; a flag is never a fraud finding. Duplicate refusals remain inspectable without creating a counted release.

| Exception | Required route |
|---|---|
| Missing source/input/resolution; extraction failure | Retain uploaded evidence and permitted registration/intake facts; keep confirmation awaiting required inputs. Supply real evidence; manual confirmation against uploaded source if extraction fails. Optional absent evidence remains absent; no fake file/reference/value. |
| Duplicate release document | Refuse independent recount and retain refusal/flag. A linked transcription correction may reuse its source; changed authority needs amended evidence. NTA is support, not another release. |
| Referral or late response | Fresh referral deadline; answer or explicit timeout returns through checks even after intake closes. Pre-final late response triggers recheck, never changes old snapshot. Final decision closes thread; resubmission creates new thread. |
| Residence disputed / lost contact | Suspend residents-list access without automatic claim decline. DSWD documents resolution/fact correction or ID-checked account/contact relink; old submission facts remain. Secure credential recovery remains Pending. |
| Unpaid input/category change | Append documented version/reason, recalculate, notify old/new, hold affected entitlements, invalidate old attempts/codes immediately. Require current confirmed successor list and affected session update before fresh recipient authorization. Unrelated list additions preserve unchanged entitlement codes. |
| Challenge before / after handover | Private own-barangay report to DSWD. Substantiated pre-handover challenge appends approval reversal/cancellation/invalidation and successor list. After handover keep Auditor evidence only; no reversal. Race resolves atomically according to whether handover was already recorded. |
| Paid amount differs from later basis | Keep handed-over input/category/amount fixed regardless of full/adverse/unconfirmed outcome. Show hypothetical shortfall/overpayment for human handling; no automatic payment/recovery. |
| Connection or acknowledgement lost | No offline queue. Restore application connection and read same transaction before retry. Recorded receipt blocks another payout. Uncertain chain submission reconciles same commitment; it never repeats a business action. |
| Notice delivery fails / no contact | Persist labelled simulated failure/no-contact in evaluation and expose operational follow-up to DSWD. Real notice delivery remains Pending; staff arrange notice outside the app. |
| Chain/evidence failure | Explicit pending/not-anchored/unreachable/absent/mismatch/unavailable states. Retry exact saved content. Funding still counts, recorded cash stays consumed, list remains pending until confirmed. Never substitute a local hash for a chain read. |

## Business state machines

Moves not listed are prohibited. Every saved move appends business history; current statuses are projections. Human moves require selected active eligible actor and current versions; system moves retain triggering event/deadline.

| Record | Permitted transitions and guards |
|---|---|
| Staff nomination/activation | `pending → active → deactivated`, DSWD with documented nomination for barangay staff; retain nominator/entrant/approver separately. No automatic reactivation or deletion. |
| Claim operation | `not_open → open → closed`, DSWD explicit coverage/basis/duration. No automatic opening or reopening in MVP. |
| Residence | `unconfirmed → confirmed/disputed`; `confirmed → disputed`; `disputed → confirmed` after DSWD resolution. Evidence/versions retained; new dispute affects list access, not automatic eligibility. |
| Claim | `waiting_barangay → awaiting_dswd` via signed/fallback route; `awaiting_dswd → referred → awaiting_dswd` via answer/timeout; `awaiting_dswd → approved/declined` by human; `declined → waiting_barangay` once/open intake/new information; `approved → reversed` only substantiated pre-handover challenge. Category changes append decisions without reopening eligibility. No automatic reapproval of reversal. |
| Response/submission | Replies append until final approval/decline; fixed signed/fallback snapshots never change. A referral keeps review open; new submission has its own thread/deadline. |
| Funding | Draft upload/confirmation → immutable recorded entry, counts immediately. Correction appends linked reversal+replacement atomically. Original superseded meaning is derived; no edit/delete. |
| Approved list | Computable candidate → `pending_publication → published`; previous published → `historical` when successor publishes. A stale pending candidate becomes `stale`, even if later anchored. Publication requires own anchor confirmation and still-current candidate. |
| Entitlement | Approval → `awaiting_amount → awaiting_publication → available`; `available → in_transaction → handed_over`; unpaid → `held/awaiting_publication` for stop/change; `held → available` only after issue/prerequisite clearance; unused expiry → `available` if still current, otherwise held; unpaid → `cancelled` by reversal. `handed_over` is terminal for consumption. |
| Session | `scheduled → open → closed`; `scheduled/open → cancelled`; same-state amendment preserves prior versions and invalidates affected unused authorizations. Closed/cancelled follow-up assignment/notes allowed; no cash reopening. |
| Attempt | `open → presented` after valid ID comparison; `open/presented → stopped` with failed-ID reason, or `expired` with code/session/change reason; `presented → handed_over` once with code/current checks. Stopped/expired are terminal attempts; retry appends a new attempt under same transaction. |
| Receipt outcome | Recorded handover → answer `received_full/received_less/not_received`, or system `unconfirmed` after 72h/no answer. `unconfirmed → answer`; any answer → appended revised answer. No outcome before handover and no outcome → payable move. |
| Grievance | Absent → `open`; DSWD `open → under_review → closed` with resolution; new adverse evidence `closed → open`. Notes/reports while open/review preserve history; full answer does not close. Pre-handover private report may use stable transaction identity without fabricating receipt/outcome. |
| Challenge | Private `open → under_review → closed` by DSWD with substantiated/dismissed resolution; consequences depend on handover already recorded. History remains. |

|---|---|

A whole-record evidence bundle may be served only where every contained field is permitted; redaction cannot preserve its hash. Selective resident inclusion proofs are Pending.

## Reliability, privacy and completion

Persist business action + business audit + required anchor intent atomically. Upload storage is not part of that database transaction: retain exact bytes before linking a successful business save; quarantine unreferenced uploads, never count partial actions. Idempotency keys and expected revisions protect retries; database uniqueness plus transactional checks enforce one entitlement/transaction/handover and atomic corrections. Recheck all current authorization immediately at handover. Never hold business locks during RPC/OCR work.

Retain immutable facts/versions/files and explicit mutable current projections. Recovery processes overdue deadlines, code expiry and 72-hour outcomes after restart; timers are persistent business rules, not browser animations. A chain outage after confirmed publication may delay receipt anchoring while an online application records handover once; it never permits offline payout. A detected lost/replaced deployment requires explicit recovery/fresh evaluation namespace, preserving old history.

Evidence must be retrievable throughout evaluation, with database/files/deployment manifests backed up and restore-tested together. Lost chain state cannot be recreated from a database backup. Show component availability and per-job failure without losing business facts. Operational latency, capacity, RPO/RTO and retention are Pending institutional requirements; no SLA is asserted. Evaluation includes overdue restart and concurrent requests, not only happy-path UI clicks.

Private records/originals stay off-chain and outside public static directories; every projected/file/material read applies selected-actor scope. ID/photo Auditor access is logged before serving; failure to persist that access blocks disclosure. Auditor reads/rechecks never emit shared inspection events. Broad access is evidence access, not access to secrets. Privacy safeguards for evaluation do not protect real records against impersonation/workstation administrators. Public commitment contents exclude identity, DFC numbers, contacts, documents, answer labels, reasons, codes/PINs/credential hashes.
